PROJECT CONTEXT
"""
FUGA+ is an individual Systems Engineering project to be developed over a 12-week period.
The identified problem is that many people incur small expenses throughout the day and may later fail to clearly recall where their money went. Furthermore, manually recording each expense can be tedious and make it difficult to consistently track spending habits.
The target users are initially young people and students who wish to organize their daily expenses and better understand their spending habits without using complex financial tools.
FUGA+ will allow users to record expenses using natural language text. For example: “Today I spent $8,000 on a taxi.”
Artificial Intelligence will interpret and classify the expense information, identifying elements such as amount, category, and description.
Expenses will be stored in a history log and can be viewed via a simple dashboard.
The system will analyze expenses using custom rules to identify potential money leaks, primarily those related to small, repetitive expenditures.
The MVP will include text-based expense logging, AI-driven interpretation and classification, storage and history, a simple dashboard, identification of potential leaks via custom rules, and basic lookup of recorded expenses.
Voice functionality may be evaluated as an additional feature if project time permits.
Excluded from the MVP are connections to bank accounts, payments, transfers, card management, credit facilities, loans, investments, professional financial advice, training a proprietary AI model, investment or market prediction, real third-party banking data, advanced notifications, advanced receipt scanning, and external banking integrations.
"""
